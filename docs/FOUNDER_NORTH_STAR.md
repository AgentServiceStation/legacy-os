# Legacy OS — Founder North Star

## Product identity
Legacy OS is an AI operating system for creative professionals, built first around Joshua DeMiguel and Legacy Lines Tattoo.

It is not merely a CRM, booking system, project manager, design application, chatbot, content scheduler, or AI wrapper. Those are capabilities inside a larger intelligence and orchestration system.

## North Star
Legacy OS should know the professional, the client, the project, the craft, the business, the history, and the next best action.

For the first user, it should feel like a trustworthy "Jarvis for a tattoo artist": context + memory + reasoning + preparation + controlled execution.

## Core loop
CAPTURE → STRUCTURE → CONNECT → UNDERSTAND → PRIORITIZE → ACT → MEASURE → LEARN → REUSE

## Product truths
- Client is the long-term relationship. Project is the operational spine for one piece of work.
- Universal capture must reduce screen-by-screen re-entry. The creator should be able to speak, paste, upload, forward, or connect information and let Legacy structure it.
- The AI Chief of Staff is the primary orchestration surface. Specialist capabilities may exist underneath without forcing users into many agent personalities.
- Legacy owns persistent memory, data, relationships, workflow state, permissions, history, provenance, and audit. Models are replaceable reasoning providers.
- Professional Operating Intelligence and Professional Craft Intelligence work together.
- Professional Craft Intelligence must preserve sources, context, disagreement, uncertainty, and outcomes instead of flattening mentor/course guidance into false consensus.
- Internal/reversible organization may run automatically. Consequential external communication, scheduling promises, publishing, payments, destructive actions, and final creative/business authority remain permission/approval-gated.
- Every meaningful AI recommendation must be grounded in inspectable evidence and provenance.
- Learning requires new evidence or changed understanding; repeated execution is not learning.
- Design approvals bind to an exact immutable version/artifact.
- Internal/private data and client-visible data must be structurally separated at the data/API layer.
- Test/sandbox data and accidental duplicates must not contaminate business analytics or learning.
- Tattoo-native depth comes first. Future creator verticals should reuse the core operating architecture without weakening the tattoo product.
- Real Legacy Lines projects are the primary test harness.

## Definition of done for v1
A real Legacy Lines tattoo project can move through the full lifecycle without duplicate manual entry, privacy leakage, fake intelligence, ambiguous approval state, or lost context. The Chief of Staff can prepare and coordinate work across the same source of truth, external actions respect owner permissions, outcomes return to the knowledge system, and the system is usable from Joshua's phone without requiring him to operate database screens all day.

## Build rule
Do not redesign working foundations for novelty. Audit → preserve → stabilize → connect → extend.
All active development happens off `main`; `main` remains the stable baseline until reviewed/tested changes are deliberately merged.
